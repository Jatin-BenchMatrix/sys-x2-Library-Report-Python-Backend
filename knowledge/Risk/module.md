# Module: Risk

## Description
The Risk module is the RCSA risk register — the operational risks identified across the
organisation, each with a category, type, owning department, and inherent/residual rating.

## Purpose
Maintain the risk register and report on risk distribution, ratings, and coverage.

## Business meaning
- A **Risk** is a single risk-register entry.
- It belongs to a **department**, has a **risk category** and **risk type**, a **status**,
  and an **inherent** and **residual** rating (assessment scale).

## Main entity
`rcsa_risk` (alias suggestion: `r`)
